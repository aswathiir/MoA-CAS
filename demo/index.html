Mixture-of-Adapters for Code-Switched ASR. A frozen IndicConformer speech model never changes. Small Hindi adapters sit inside it, and a frame-level router decides, every 40 ms, how much of the adapter to use, so a speaker can switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence. Pick an utterance and compare what the base model, the adapter alone, and the router each hear.
